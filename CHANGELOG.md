# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-20

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Makes Minecraft faster where no other mod does, without
changing anything but speed.

- Instant world exit: back to the menu right away when leaving a singleplayer world; the world keeps
  saving in the background.
- Faster item frames: bases full of item frames draw about 20% faster per frame (about 25% without
  Sodium), looking exactly as in vanilla.
- Settings screen to switch each optimization on or off (Mod Menu supported).
